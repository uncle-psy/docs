## What this site documents

A **Kiduna** is a West Virginia Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association — a member-governed institution organized around at least one hundred Members, a common purpose, and governing principles that can include blockchain programs, consensus algorithms, and policy proposals. The form was created so that human creativity and intelligent agents could coordinate at scale without surrendering accountability to a platform.

This documentation describes the conceptual architecture, the institutional design, and the technical stack that lets people form Kidunas, configure Allies, build Alliances, and govern with legal standing.

## What's in the system

A Kiduna is built from six institutional layers — Legal, Governance, Identity, Economy, Memory, Interface — and supported by a technical stack of Kiduna Codes, FROST threshold wallets, the Kiduna Graph, governance markets, Offerings, and the Kiduna Protocol. Three product surfaces sit on top: **Kiduna Club** (the end-user app), **Kiduna Studio** (where Allies, Missions, and organizations are built), and **Kiduna Exchange** (the marketplace).

See [Anatomy of a Kiduna](/concepts/six-layers) and [The Kiduna stack](/builders/stack) for detail.
