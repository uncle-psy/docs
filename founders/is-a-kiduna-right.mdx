## What this page covers

- The conditions under which a Kiduna is the right form
- Cases where another form (LLC, traditional nonprofit, DAO without DUNA wrapper, informal community) makes more sense
- A short decision checklist

## Outline

### When a Kiduna fits
- Member-governed institution
- ≥100 prospective Members aligned around a Mission
- Want legal standing, treasury, and agentic infrastructure together
- Comfortable with WV jurisdiction and DUNA Act conditions

### When it doesn't
- Closely held private venture with no cooperative dimension
- Single-founder business with employees, not Members
- Ad hoc group that doesn't need treasury or governance
- Existing nonprofit with no agentic component planned

A short list of yes/no questions to clarify fit.
