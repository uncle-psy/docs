## What this page covers

- The contractual relationship between a Sponsor and a Kiduna or Alliance
- Standard template structure
- Key terms typically negotiated
- How agreements interact with the Kiduna's governing principles
- Dispute resolution

## Outline

### Contractual standing
A Kiduna can enter contracts under its legal standing as a DUNA. Sponsorship agreements are ordinary contracts entered by the Kiduna (or by an Alliance under a Kiduna) with the Sponsor.

### Standard template structure
Anticipated structure for a Kiduna sponsorship agreement: parties and standing, scope, term, deliverables, fees and consideration, attribution and publicity rights, exclusivity, termination, dispute resolution. Specific templates will be published once they're drafted.

### Key terms typically negotiated
Scope of publicity. Categories of Offerings. Editorial control. Exclusivity. Term length and renewal. Revenue share if applicable. Audit and reporting.

### Interaction with governing principles
Sponsorship terms cannot conflict with the Kiduna's Mission or governing principles. Where they touch policy-relevant decisions, agreements may need to reference enacted policy proposals.

### Dispute resolution
Where complaints go, how mediation or arbitration is invoked, what role the Kiduna's institutional dispute process plays.
