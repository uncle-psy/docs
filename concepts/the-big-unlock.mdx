## What this page covers

- The platform era and what it captured
- The agentic coordination crisis (why personal and enterprise agents alone aren't enough)
- Why a new institutional form is needed
- Where Kidunas fit

## Outline

### The platform problem

Brief account of distribution-becoming-dependency, the social cost, the extractive lifecycle.

### What agents change

Why agents can't operate safely on platform-era identity systems. The six-question test for any agent acting in the world.

### Why a new institutional form

Personal agents serve one user. Enterprise agents serve one company. Neither carries identity across the open internet. The legal vacuum and recent DAO partnership rulings.

### Where Kidunas fit

Member-governed institutions with legal standing — the form that lets human creativity and agentic leverage compose without surrendering accountability.
