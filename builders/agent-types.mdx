## Outline

### Ally
Represents a Member, Kiduna, Mission, or Alliance in relationship. Bound by Code, policy, scope, and the Kiduna's standing. Relational. Identity-bearing.

### Performer
Executes bounded tasks — posting, scheduling, transacting, routing, completing workflows. Acts only within authority granted by an Ally, Program, or policy. Task-scoped. Stateless across tasks.

### Envoy
Participates in governance markets on behalf of a Member, according to Member-defined values, constraints, and budget. Inspectable and revocable by the Member it represents.

### Program
Maintains continuity of a treasury, policy surface, Mission, workflow, or Kiduna context. Governed by institutional policy and Member-approved changes. Durable, long-running.
