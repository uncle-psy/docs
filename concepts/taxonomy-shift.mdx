## What this page covers

- The mapping from platform-era to Kiduna-era concepts
- Why each shift matters beyond cosmetics
- What this means for product, software, law, and finance categories

## Outline

### The mapping
| Platform era | Kiduna era | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| Users | Members | Account-holder becomes participant in a registered institution. |
| Audiences | Members | Relationship sits inside an institution rather than in front of a platform. |
| Followers | Participants | Social attachment becomes active contribution. |
| Creators renting distribution | Creators owning institutions | Body of work becomes treasury, governance system, Member community. |
| Engagement | Allegiance | The system asks whom it serves and who accepts responsibility. |
| Feeds | Vibes | Surface becomes a persistent relational environment with visible context. |
| Bots / personal agents | Accountable Allies | Software actors carry authority, policy, revocable scope. |
| Monetization | Reciprocal circulation | Value routes through Members, treasuries, contributors, Mission. |
| Platform control | Legal standing | Authority resolves to a recognized institution, not a private interface. |

### Why these shifts aren't cosmetic
Each row reflects a difference in how software, law, moderation, advertising, product, and finance treat the entity. A user is governed differently than a Member.

### Implications for the codebase
This mapping should guide variable names, table names, and external API surface. The [Glossary](/reference/glossary) is the authoritative source.
