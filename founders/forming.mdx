## Outline

### Statutory requirements
WV Code §31D-1-101 et seq. Article 13 of Chapter 36 effective July 1, 2026. DUNA defined as an unincorporated nonprofit association with at least 100 members joined by mutual consent for a common nonprofit purpose, elected to be formed under the Act.

### What the form gives you
Standing as a separate legal entity. Capacity to hold property, enter contracts, sue and be sued, govern its members, appear in proceedings.

### What it requires
Service-of-process agent. Public legal home. Governing principles documented and machine-readable where applicable.
