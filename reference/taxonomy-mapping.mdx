## What this page covers

This is a flat reference table for fast lookup. For the why behind each shift, see [The shifting taxonomy](/concepts/taxonomy-shift). For authoritative definitions, see the [Glossary](/reference/glossary).

## The mapping

| Platform era | Kiduna era | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| Users | Members | Account-holder becomes participant in a registered institution. |
| Audiences | Members | Relationship sits inside an institution rather than in front of a platform. |
| Followers | Participants | Social attachment becomes active contribution and governance. |
| Creators renting distribution | Creators owning institutions | The body of work becomes a treasury, governance system, and Member community. |
| Engagement | Allegiance | The system asks whom it serves and who accepts responsibility. |
| Feeds | Vibes | The surface becomes a persistent relational environment with visible context. |
| Bots / personal agents | Accountable Allies | Software actors carry authority, policy, and revocable scope. |
| Monetization | Reciprocal circulation | Value routes through Members, treasuries, contributors, and the Mission. |
| Platform control | Legal standing | Authority resolves to a recognized institution rather than a private interface. |
| Terms of service | Governing principles | Rules are member-adopted and machine-readable, not imposed unilaterally. |
| Account ban | Membership revocation | Removal follows institutional process, not platform discretion. |
