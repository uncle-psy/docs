## What this page covers

- Each primitive defined
- The resolution rule: every relationship resolves to an Alliance; every Alliance resolves to a Kiduna; every Kiduna resolves to legal standing
- How the primitives compose in practice

## Outline

### Members
Who they are, what they can do, the human / non-human question.

### Allies
The relational face of agentic action.

### Alliances
How coordination across Members, Allies, and institutions takes shape.

### Vibes
The persistent surface where Alliances live.

### Missions
The purpose around which a Kiduna organizes.

### Offerings
The transferable economic objects that circulate through the system.

### The resolution pattern
Every relationship → Alliance → Kiduna → legal standing.
