## What this page covers

- What an Ally is and how it relates to you
- The inform / instruct / empower / align process
- Scope, policy, and revocable authority
- Kiduna Codes that bind the Ally
- Reviewing and revising your Ally over time

## Outline

### What your Ally is
A software actor that represents you in relationship. Bound by Code, policy, scope, and the Kiduna's standing.

### Inform, instruct, empower, align
The four phases of Ally configuration.

### Scope and authority
What your Ally can do and where the limits are.

### Codes
How the Ally's identity, scope, expiry, and revocation are encoded.

### Review and revision
Inspecting your Ally's actions. Revising scope. Revoking authority.
