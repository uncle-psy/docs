## Outline

### The stack at a glance
Kiduna Codes (JWT identity / scope), FROST threshold wallets (signing), Kiduna Graph (memory), Governance markets (decision-making), Offerings (economic objects), Kiduna Protocol (settlement). Six pieces; they compose.
