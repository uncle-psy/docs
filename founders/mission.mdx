## What this page covers

- What a Mission is and isn't
- Mission as legal requirement (DUNA Act)
- Drafting a Mission that scopes governance, treasury, and Allies
- How Mission evolves through enacted policy proposals

## Outline

### What the Mission is
The common nonprofit purpose required by the DUNA Act, and the durable orienting commitment of the institution.

### What the Mission isn't
A tagline, a brand statement, or a vision document. The Mission is operational.

### Drafting principles
Concrete enough to scope action, broad enough to last.

### Mission and governance
How Mission constrains what Members can vote to do.

### Mission evolution
How and when a Mission can be revised through enacted policy proposals.
