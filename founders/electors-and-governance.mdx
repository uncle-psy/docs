## What this page covers

- The Elector role and how Electors are appointed or chosen
- How governance markets work (conditional pass/fail)
- The Envoy agent type and Member delegation to Envoys
- Human oversight requirements and constraints

## Outline

### Electors
Defined governance roles named in the institution's records. Scope, term, accountability.

### Governance markets
Conditional pass/fail markets that price the expected consequence of each proposal. How resolution works. Why this is more reliable than vote-counting alone.

### Envoys
Member-configured agents that participate in markets on behalf of the Member, within defined values, constraints, and budget. Inspectable and revocable.

### Human oversight
What can be automated and what cannot. Where the institution requires a human in the loop.
