## What this page covers

- Kiduna Club: the end-user app where Members participate
- Kiduna Studio: the builder surface where Allies and institutions are designed
- Kiduna Exchange: the marketplace for agents, goods, and services
- How a typical Member moves across all three

## Outline

### Kiduna Club
End-user app. Mobile, desktop, web, CTV. Join Kidunas, configure Allies, participate in Vibes, hold wallets / Codes / Coins.

### Kiduna Studio
Builder and creator surface. Design Allies through inform / instruct / empower / align. Form organizations. Draft governing principles. Design Missions, Vibes, Offerings. Define policies, scopes, Ally Codes.

### Kiduna Exchange
Marketplace. Discover Kidunas and Offerings. Buy and license agents, components, templates. Integrated affiliate marketing. Provenance and attribution travel with every trade.

### Cross-surface flow
A worked example of a Member discovering a Kiduna on Exchange, joining via Club, configuring an Ally in Studio.
