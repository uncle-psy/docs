## What this page covers

- The three levels of agency (personal, Alliance, Kiduna)
- The six conditions of agency in detail
- Why platforms separate them and Kidunas compose them

## Outline

### Three levels of agency
Personal agency. Alliance agency. Kiduna agency. These are not separate products; they are one architecture viewed at different scales.

### The six conditions
- **Transparency / Perception.** Seeing the actual field of action.
- **Purpose / Intention.** Deciding what is worth doing and why.
- **Governance / Authority.** The right to act, the scope of delegation.
- **Leverage.** Tools, wallets, ledgers, memory, workflows, codes, models, protocols.
- **Coordination.** Acting with others across boundaries without losing context.
- **Accountability / Consequence.** Records, treasury movements, governance decisions, repair, liability.

### What composition makes possible
A worked example of how the six conditions interact in a single Member's action through an Ally, mediated by a Kiduna.
