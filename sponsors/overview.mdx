## What this page covers

- What a Sponsor is
- How sponsorship differs from Membership
- The kinds of value a Sponsor can contribute and receive
- Where sponsorship attaches in the Kiduna architecture

## Outline

### What a Sponsor is
A Sponsor is an external entity — a business, nonprofit, organization, or individual — that has a contractual or economic relationship with a Kiduna or one of its Alliances. Sponsors are not Members. They participate economically and contractually but do not vote, propose, or hold Member roles.

### How sponsorship differs from Membership
| | Member | Sponsor |
|---|---|---|
| Governance rights | Yes | No |
| Holds Membership fee / Coins | Yes | Not required |
| Can propose and vote | Yes | No |
| Relationship | Internal | External |
| Primary contribution | Participation, governance, fees | Economic, contractual, in-kind |
| Primary benefit | Access, voice, ownership in outcomes | Publicity, distribution, association |

### What a Sponsor contributes
Examples: sponsorship fees, in-kind support, services, equipment, distribution access, expertise, branded content.

### What a Sponsor receives
Examples: publicity within a Vibe, the right to sell specific Offerings, brand association, domain positioning, attribution in Alliance records.

### Where sponsorship attaches
Sponsorship can attach to a Kiduna as a whole or to a specific Alliance under that Kiduna. The pitch deck shows Sponsors associated with Alliance domains (Creative, Civic, Therapeutic, Educational, Scientific, Economic, Family, Service, Infrastructure). The canonical scope is still being decided — see the [open question in the Glossary](/reference/glossary#sponsor).
