### Initial inquiry
How to reach out. Who at the Kiduna handles inquiries. What basic information to provide.

### Evaluation
What the Kiduna considers — Mission fit, sector alignment, conflicts with existing Sponsors, Member sentiment, compliance fit.

### Negotiation
Standard versus custom terms. Who has authority to negotiate. Where Member governance enters.

### Signing
How agreements are executed. Service-of-process considerations. Effective date.

### During the term
Reporting, regular check-ins, content deliveries, attribution verification.
