## What this page covers

- Each of the six layers and what it provides
- How the layers interact
- What older institutions kept separate that the Kiduna composes

## Outline

### Legal
Standing, service of process, property, liability boundaries, proceedings, jurisdiction.

### Governance
Members, proposals, policies, Electors, decision markets, human oversight.

### Identity
Kiduna Codes, roles, scopes, revocation, lineage, authority.

### Economy
Treasuries, Offerings, Coins, stable settlement, fees, circulation.

### Memory
The Kiduna Graph — records, provenance, relationships, outcomes, continuity.

### Interface
Living dynamic spaces, conversations, games, dashboards, ceremonies, operational surfaces.

### How they interact
Cross-layer flow examples: a proposal moves through Governance, settles in Treasury (Economy), is recorded in Memory, surfaces in Interface, and is enforced through Identity.
