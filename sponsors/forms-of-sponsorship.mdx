## What this page covers

- The three primary forms of sponsorship currently anticipated
- Hybrid and custom arrangements
- How a Sponsor and Kiduna decide which form applies

## Outline

### Publicity sponsorship
Sponsor pays for visibility within a Vibe, on a Kiduna's interface surfaces, or in association with an Alliance's activities. Standard placements, durations, and editorial constraints will be defined here.

### Offering sponsorship
Sponsor receives the right to sell specific Offerings within or through a Kiduna or Alliance. Revenue share terms, attribution requirements, and exclusivity questions live here.

### Services sponsorship
Sponsor provides services (legal, technical, professional, infrastructure, distribution, etc.) to the Kiduna or Alliance in exchange for sponsorship benefits.

### Hybrid arrangements
Most real sponsorship arrangements combine elements of more than one form. How to structure these without losing legibility.
